# Contribution Guidelines

Thanks for helping improve this list.

## What qualifies

Superintelligence must be the subject of the resource, not merely its motivation. A resource qualifies if it is centrally about:

- AI that far exceeds human ability, whether broadly or within a specific domain.
- The intelligence explosion or recursive self-improvement.
- Aligning, controlling, or governing systems smarter than humans.

General AI safety, interpretability, evals, and ML tooling don't qualify on their own. Well-argued skeptical views of superintelligence are welcome.

## Adding an entry

- Add one resource per pull request.
- Put it in the most fitting section. All sections except Research Organizations are ordered by publication date, oldest first. Research Organizations are ordered alphabetically.
- Use the format `- [Name](link) (Year) - Description.` Organizations don't take a year.
- Keep the description short, starting with a capital letter and ending with a period.
- Link to the original source where possible.
- Run [awesome-lint](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome-lint) before submitting.
