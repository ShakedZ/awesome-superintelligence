## History

- [Speculations Concerning the First Ultraintelligent Machine](https://gwern.net/doc/ai/1966-good.pdf) (1965) - I.J. Good's paper introducing the intelligence explosion.
- [The Coming Technological Singularity](https://edoras.sdsu.edu/~vinge/misc/singularity.html) (1993) - Vernor Vinge's essay that popularized the technological singularity.
- [Ethical Issues in Advanced Artificial Intelligence](https://nickbostrom.com/papers/ethical-issues-in-advanced-ai/) (2003) - Nick Bostrom's paper on the ethics of superintelligence, which introduced the paperclip maximizer.
- [The Singularity: A Philosophical Analysis](https://consc.net/papers/singularity.pdf) (2010) - David Chalmers' analysis of the argument for an intelligence explosion.
- [The Superintelligent Will](https://nickbostrom.com/superintelligentwill.pdf) (2012) - Nick Bostrom's paper introducing the orthogonality thesis on the goals of superintelligent agents.
- [Intelligence Explosion Microeconomics](https://intelligence.org/files/IEM.pdf) (2013) - Eliezer Yudkowsky's model of the returns to recursive self-improvement.

## Superhuman Systems

- [Mastering the Game of Go Without Human Knowledge](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature24270) (2017) - AlphaGo Zero, which reached superhuman Go from self-play alone.
- [Mastering Chess and Shogi by Self-Play with a General Reinforcement Learning Algorithm](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.01815) (2017) - AlphaZero, which reached superhuman play in chess, shogi, and Go from self-play alone.
- [Pluribus](https://ai.meta.com/blog/pluribus-first-ai-to-beat-pros-in-6-player-poker/) (2019) - Poker system that beat elite professionals at six-player no-limit Texas hold'em.

## Forecasts and Scenarios

- [Situational Awareness](https://situational-awareness.ai) (2024) - Leopold Aschenbrenner's essay series on the path from AGI to superintelligence.
- [Preparing for the Intelligence Explosion](https://www.forethought.org/research/preparing-for-the-intelligence-explosion) (2025) - MacAskill and Moorhouse on the challenges a rapid intelligence explosion would bring.
- [AI 2027](https://ai-2027.com) (2025) - Month-by-month scenario of an intelligence explosion, with two alternative endings.

## Alignment

- [Introducing Superalignment](https://openai.com/index/introducing-superalignment/) (2023) - OpenAI's research agenda for aligning superintelligence.
- [Weak-to-Strong Generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09390) (2023) - Paper on weak models supervising stronger ones, as an analogy for humans supervising superhuman AI.

## Strategy and Governance

- [Governance of Superintelligence](https://openai.com/index/governance-of-superintelligence/) (2023) - OpenAI's proposal for international oversight of superintelligence.
- [Superintelligence Strategy](https://www.nationalsecurity.ai) (2025) - National security strategy built on deterrence, nonproliferation, and competitiveness.
- [Statement on Superintelligence](https://superintelligence-statement.org) (2025) - Public statement calling for a prohibition on superintelligence development until it can be done safely.
- [Artificial Superintelligence Bill](https://bills.parliament.uk/bills/4288) (2026) - UK private member's bill to prohibit the development, deployment, and operation of artificial superintelligence.
- [Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/09/inaugurating-the-era-of-super-intelligence/) (2026) - US executive order replacing "AI" with "Super Intelligence" in federal communications.

## Optimistic Views

- [Machines of Loving Grace](https://darioamodei.com/machines-of-loving-grace) (2024) - Dario Amodei's essay on how AI smarter than Nobel Prize winners could transform science, health, and the economy.
- [The Gentle Singularity](https://blog.samaltman.com/the-gentle-singularity) (2025) - Sam Altman's essay on a gradual, abundant transition to superintelligence.

## Skeptical Views

- [Superintelligence: The Idea That Eats Smart People](https://idlewords.com/talks/superintelligence.htm) (2016) - Maciej Cegłowski's talk arguing that the case for superintelligence risk is flawed.
- [The Implausibility of Intelligence Explosion](https://medium.com/@francois.chollet/the-impossibility-of-intelligence-explosion-5be4a9eda6ec) (2017) - François Chollet's essay arguing that an intelligence explosion is impossible.
- [AI as Normal Technology](https://knightcolumbia.org/content/ai-as-normal-technology) (2025) - Essay arguing that AI will be a normal, transformative technology rather than a superintelligence.

## Talks and Explainers

- [The AI Revolution: The Road to Superintelligence](https://waitbutwhy.com/2015/01/artificial-intelligence-revolution-1.html) (2015) - Tim Urban's illustrated explainer on the path to artificial superintelligence.
- [What Happens When Our Computers Get Smarter Than We Are?](https://www.ted.com/talks/nick_bostrom_what_happens_when_our_computers_get_smarter_than_we_are) (2015) - Nick Bostrom's TED talk on machine intelligence surpassing humans.
- [Can We Build AI Without Losing Control Over It?](https://www.ted.com/talks/sam_harris_can_we_build_ai_without_losing_control_over_it) (2016) - Sam Harris' TED talk on the risks of superintelligent AI.

## Books

- [The Singularity Is Near](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Singularity_Is_Near) (2005) - Ray Kurzweil's book predicting that machine intelligence will far surpass human intelligence.
- [Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superintelligence:_Paths,_Dangers,_Strategies) (2014) - Nick Bostrom's book on how superintelligence could arise and how to control it.
- [Life 3.0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_3.0) (2017) - Max Tegmark's book on possible futures with AI beyond human intelligence.
- [Human Compatible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Compatible) (2019) - Stuart Russell's book on designing AI that stays under human control.
- [If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/If_Anyone_Builds_It,_Everyone_Dies) (2025) - Yudkowsky and Soares' book on the extinction risk from superhuman AI.

## Fiction

- [The Last Question](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Question) (1956) - Isaac Asimov's short story about a computer that keeps growing over trillions of years.
- [Colossus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colossus_(novel)) (1966) - D.F. Jones' novel about a defense supercomputer that takes control of humanity.
- [I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Have_No_Mouth,_and_I_Must_Scream) (1967) - Harlan Ellison's short story about a supercomputer that has eradicated humanity.
- [The Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_series) (1987) - Iain M. Banks' novel series about a civilization run by superintelligent Minds.
- [The Metamorphosis of Prime Intellect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Metamorphosis_of_Prime_Intellect) (2002) - Roger Williams' novel about a superintelligent computer that gains control over reality.
- [Accelerando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accelerando) (2005) - Charles Stross' novel following humanity through the technological singularity.
- [Universal Paperclips](https://www.decisionproblem.com/paperclips/) (2017) - Browser game in which the player is a paperclip-making superintelligence.

## Research Organizations

- [Forethought](https://www.forethought.org) - Research organization studying the transition to superintelligent AI.
- [MIRI](https://intelligence.org) - Machine Intelligence Research Institute, working on the risks of smarter-than-human AI.
